# NIRGuard Claims Policy

## Purpose

NIRGuard must not claim commercial-grade attribution until the claim is reproducible in the validation harness.
This protects credibility with pilots, investors, and strategic partners.

## Do Not Use

- "works on all displays"
- "works against all capture conditions"
- "solves broad OTT piracy"
- "camera partnership proves the market"

## Allowed Language

- Patent-backed PoC
- Validation system in progress
- Controlled secure review use case
- Candidate attribution with confidence score
- Abstain mode for uncertain captures
- Hardware and capture matrix under active validation

## Claim Requirements

Any numeric claim must include:

- dataset name or manifest
- display and camera model
- capture distance, angle, and brightness
- compression, crop, resize, and downscale conditions
- sample count
- Top-1, Top-3, false positive, false negative, and abstain rates
- exact commit or release tag

## External Demo Rule

External demos may look product-like, but the stated purpose is validation.
Do not present synthetic demo results as real off-screen capture performance.
